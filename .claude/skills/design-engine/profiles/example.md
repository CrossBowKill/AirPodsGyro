## What it is
Tidewell is a phone app that tells sea swimmers when a beach is safe to swim, using tide tables and the local water temperature.

## Audience and the one action
- Audience: year-round sea swimmers, most of them over 40, checking on a phone at the car park.
- Default action: download the app.

## Hard rules
Any breach is an automatic FAIL from the juror.
- Never say a beach "is safe". Say what the tide and the water are doing, and let the swimmer decide.
- Never show a swimmer without a tow float.
- No lifeguard, coastguard or council logos.
