# Ad Structure Framework

## Purpose

Structures, not aesthetics. The research method behind the Video Ad Engine: mine the ad libraries for the MOST DUPLICATED and longest-running ads in adjacent markets (duplication and longevity are the performance signal), extract their structures, and build concepts on a two-layer architecture. Loaded by `ad-strategist` when briefing Novel concepts and by `ad-trend-scout` when classifying what it finds. There is no universal best structure; steal from proven neighbours.

**Category**: Branded content / Copywriting

## Inputs

- The trend pool (`video-ads/trends/latest.md`) and any ad-library teardown notes
- The product's TRUE claims and real artifacts (what can honestly be proven on screen)
- The 8-beat narrative (RECIPE.md section 1b) that every scripted concept maps onto

## System Context

Read `video-ads/foundations/PLAYBOOK.md`, `RECIPE.md` section 1b, and `.Codex/skills/content/ad-hook-library/SKILL.md`.

## The research method

1. In an ad-intelligence library (preferred) or the Meta Ad Library, filter your category's adjacent markets (AI tools, developer tools, SaaS, digital products, lifetime deals).
2. Sort by run length and by the number of near-identical duplicates an advertiser is running. Duplication = the advertiser's own proof it converts.
3. For each long-runner, write down the STRUCTURE (beat order, where the product enters, where the proof sits, the close), not the look.
4. Map each structure onto your product's true claims. Discard any that would need a false claim.

## The master two-layer architecture (applies to every concept)

```
LAYER 1 (0-2s)   PATTERN INTERRUPT: a premium object or scene render (anchored still, animated)
                 with the TEXT HOOK burned on frame 1 as an HTML panel. Sound-off legible.
      hard cut
LAYER 2 (2-20s)  PROOF: real product footage or a real static artifact proving exactly ONE thing,
                 in the skeptic register throughout. Labels name the mechanism as it fires.
CLOSE (20-25s)   brand and logo late, the value line, "{your price}, once", one CTA to {your domain}.
```

Rules of the architecture: 9:16, 15-30s, built sound-off first; renders are the stopper, never the proof; one hook, one visual idea, one CTA per ad; the skeptic register (self-aware, transparent, the viewer is in charge) throughout; brand late; the honest limitation somewhere in layer 2 for cold audiences.

## The 8-beat narrative rides on top

For scripted concepts the beats of the 8-beat structure (pain hook, why it happens, belief shift, mechanism, product, benefits, value stack, honest future pace) distribute across the two layers: beats 1-2 in layer 1 and the start of layer 2; beats 3-6 in layer 2; beats 7-8 in the close. Longer cuts expand beats 2-4, never beat 1.

## Five high-confidence concept shapes (templates, not briefs)

1. **The split-screen receipt** (structures 6 + 5): identical prompt both sides; left drifts (re-asks, stubs, claims done); right loads the system, flags its own draft, blocks the incomplete diff, finishes verified; freeze on the delta: "The difference is not the model. It is the system around it."; pack render, value line, CTA. Requires REAL reproducible runs.
2. **The belief-shift flagship** (structures 4 + 3): VO over terminal footage + object b-roll; the full 8 beats; the researched #1 pain verbatim as the hook.
3. **The stat open** (structure 14 + the two-layer architecture): object render with a sourced stat burned on frame 1, hard cut to ONE real proof, one idea per variant; the most duplicable shape (swap the stat, keep the skeleton).
4. **The skeptic's conversion** (structure 13): presenter or avatar at a desk, cut to the real terminal within 2s, the objection lived, the surprise shown, the limitation admitted, "but I stopped being the babysitter", stack + CTA. Stronger warm than cold.
5. **The POV identity reel** (structures 11 + 1): lo-fi first-person desk and screen, no VO, ambient audio, the assistant picks up yesterday's context and runs its own checks, caption CTA, logo last. Cheapest CPM slot; doubles as organic.

Diversification slots after the five: the comedy sketch character (structure 7), a 60-90s value-stack VSL for retargeting (structure 10), one ASMR silent run as a cheap explore slot.

## Launch shape

One campaign, each concept its own ad (one idea per ad; near-identical ads collapse into one delivery entity). Variants of the stat open are separate ads sharing a skeleton. Kill rule: under 1% CTR at 2,000 impressions per cell. The winner gets near-identical duplication with rotated hooks (the documented practice of the longest-running advertisers).

## Process

1. Pull 10-20 long-runners from your adjacent markets; write each one's structure in one line.
2. Match each to one of the 14 (or log a 15th with evidence).
3. For a Novel brief, pick a structure not used in the last 30 days, map it onto the two-layer architecture and the 8 beats, name the ONE thing the proof layer proves, and attach the hypothesis.
4. Hand the shape to the scriptwriter with the rhythm and the substance beat named.

## Output Format

```
Structure pick: #{n} {name} (lineage: {category}, {longevity evidence})
Two-layer map: L1 {render + text hook} -> L2 {the ONE proof} -> close {value line, price, CTA}
8-beat map: {beat: seconds} ...
Honesty check: every on-screen claim maps to a real capability: yes
```

## Best Practices

- Steal structures, not aesthetics. The look dates in months; the structure runs for years.
- The proof layer proves ONE thing. A demo of three things proves none.
- Duplicate the winner with rotated hooks; do not reinvent it.
- The enemy is the status quo, never a competitor or a platform by name.
